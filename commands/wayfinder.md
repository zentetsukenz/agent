---
description: Chart or work through a wayfinder map — a shared plan of decision tickets for work too big for one session.
---

Wayfind: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `wayfinder` skill. If the argument is a loose idea with no existing map, chart the map (name the destination, map the frontier, create it and its tickets). If the argument names or implies an existing map (URL, number, or "continue"/"next ticket"), work through it: load the map, claim the next frontier ticket (or the one named), resolve it, and record the resolution. Never resolve more than one ticket per session, except research tickets.
